# 3×3 光标概念图顺序

Concept sheets are read left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Never reorder cells between candidate sheets or the final selected sheet.

| Cell | Windows role | Image meaning | Hotspot |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Arrow` | Default pointer; clear arrow tip points upper-left | Near arrow tip |
| 2 | `Help` | Help pointer with a single `?` badge | Near arrow tip |
| 3 | `No` | Unavailable/error pointer with a red X badge | Near arrow tip |
| 4 | `IBeam` | Text insertion beam | Center |
| 5 | `Crosshair` | Precision crosshair | Center |
| 6 | `SizeNS` | Vertical resize, up/down | Center |
| 7 | `SizeWE` | Horizontal resize, left/right | Center |
| 8 | `SizeNESW` | Diagonal resize, southwest–northeast | Center |
| 9 | `SizeNWSE` | Diagonal resize, northwest–southeast | Center |

The 3×3 sheet supplies nine roles. Windows themes can also name `AppStarting`, `Wait`, `NWPen`, `SizeAll`, `UpArrow`, and `Hand`. Do not silently mislabel an unrelated icon to fill them. During install, preserve the current per-user assignment for roles not supplied by the generated set.

For a concept sample, vary how Yuri is integrated into the glyph, not character identity, face, palette, or pixel style. Keep each cursor role functional at 32×32.
